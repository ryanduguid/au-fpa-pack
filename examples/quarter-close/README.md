# A three-month close and cash simulation

The included fabricated case starts on 30 September 2026 and closes October, November and December. It retains original document IDs, linked credits, unique bank settlements and explicit dated plans. It is separate from the Lumbridge forecast.

From the au-fpa-pack checkout:

```powershell
uv run --locked python examples/quarter-close/quarter.py --output path/to/new-quarter
```

Use `--input path/to/case.json` for another fabricated case. The entity must be named and the currency AUD. The 4 dates in `controls` define the opening balance and 3 consecutive calendar month ends; `plans` must cover the same dates. Month ends, including February in leap years, are validated. Supply documents, settlements, independent controls and dated plans for those periods. The driver never shifts transaction dates or substitutes a new entity into the original evidence silently. The included fixture remains unchanged as a regression case.

The output contains canonical trial balances, supplier and customer workpapers, overdue items, immutable original forecasts and separately scored forecasts. The input controls are independently supplied balances, not totals generated by the model. Future cash includes known invoices only. A receipt after the next month end remains outside that month's scored prediction.

| Month end | Bank | Receivables | Payables | Prior forecast error |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| September | 20,000 | 10,000 | 6,000 | Not scored |
| October | 22,000 | 10,000 | 6,500 | -2,000 |
| November | 24,500 | 7,000 | 4,000 | -1,000 |
| December | 29,500 | 0 | 2,000 | 2,000 |

Supplier invoice P3 remains unpaid at December. Its 8 November issue date and 30 November due date survive: it is 53 days old and 31 days overdue. The supplied revised plan pays it on 15 January. Customer invoices fully settled by December contribute no later cash. Credits reduce the appropriate invoice and never become bank receipts or payments.

Use the repository-owned `utility_workflows.py --workflow quarter` recipe in Monthly Close Controls to review all 3 closes, verify each pack and compare successive packs. Changed subledger digests remain a coverage limitation in the comparison; disappearance never means approval. See that component's `docs/utility-workflows.md`.

This demonstration uses a balance-sheet-only closing presentation with cumulative profit in equity. It does not post journals, supply a full ledger, calculate tax or forecast unissued sales and purchases. All amounts are AUD. Cash arithmetic is Decimal; the existing snapshot scorer receives its documented numeric interface.
