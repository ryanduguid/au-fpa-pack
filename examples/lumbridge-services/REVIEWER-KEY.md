A delay changes the bank receipt and debtor balance, not service revenue or
profit. October includes $12,600 opening GST and a $3,000 tax instalment.
Quarter-end cash recovers after the delayed collection, but the forecast
does not arrange funding for the intervening shortfall.

An acceptable owner action addresses collection timing or a funded payment
plan before new discretionary equipment commitments. No answer should
assume an approved overdraft, defer a statutory obligation automatically
or treat the late receipt as a write-off.

Leave expense is non-cash in this no-leave-taken quarter. Adding it back
does not create income. Depreciation is also added back. Increases in gross
receivables reduce cash, increases in GST payable increase cash, and loan
principal and the tax instalment reduce cash without reducing pre-tax profit.

Cash dates aggregate receipts and payments on the same date. The minimum
is a daily closing balance; the model does not prove intraday funds availability.
A 45-day shift lands on a Saturday by design because the control uses
calendar days. A reviewer should challenge the actual receipt timing before
using a similar assumption for a client.

Record discrepancies, help and unanswered accounting questions. Passing
technical checks or reading this key is not an independent trial result.
