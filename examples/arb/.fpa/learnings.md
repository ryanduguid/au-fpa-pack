# Learnings

Export growth of 16.4% is not a company-wide rate. Australian
aftermarket was flat. Uniform growth worsens the retrospective fit.
Cost pressure (THB, US tariffs) belongs in a labelled sensitivity until a
separate evaluation supports it and a human approves promotion.

Both challengers use the FY2025 realised export growth input. Their failed
holdout-separation check prevents promotion; retrospective fit is not forecast accuracy.
